## Important distinction

The evidence directly establishes the displayed instrument readings. The
18.15 mA independently calculated value and 18.18 mA theoretical value are
derived/reference quantities, not values that need to appear on a PPK2 screen.

Likewise, the 0.22% figure is calculated from 18.14 mA measured versus
18.18 mA theoretical:
(18.18 - 18.14) / 18.18 = 0.22%.
